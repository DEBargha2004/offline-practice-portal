# Chapter 52: Smart Grid — Part II: Phasor Measurement & Security

## Core Concept
Maintaining stability across dynamic, renewable-heavy smart grids requires synchronized wide-area electrical monitoring. Phasor Measurement Units (PMUs) sample electrical waveforms at millisecond resolution, while Meter Data Management Systems coordinate consumption telemetry amidst emerging cyber-physical threats.

---

## 1. Phasor Measurement Units (PMU) & Synchrophasors
* **The Power Grid Challenge:** Alternating Current (AC) waveforms fluctuate continuously in voltage, frequency, and phase angle across interconnected transmission lines.
* **PMU (Phasor Measurement Unit):** An electrical device that measures voltage and current AC waveforms and time-stamps them using high-precision **GPS satellite clocks**.
* **Synchrophasors:** Time-synchronized phasor measurements taken from across an entire continent simultaneously.
* **Grid Reliability:** PMUs sample up to 30 to 60 times per second (compared to traditional SCADA, which samples once every 2 to 4 seconds). This allows control centers to detect inter-area power oscillations, phase angle separation, and voltage collapse seconds before blackouts occur.

---

## 2. Grid Communication & Data Aggregation Hierarchy
* **Smart Meters:** Deployed at customer premises.
* **Data Aggregation Unit (DAU):** Concentrator gathering readings from neighborhood smart meter clusters and compressing the data.
* **Meter Data Management System (MDMS):** Centralized utility software that validates, cleanses, bills, and analyzes massive AMI data streams.

---

## 3. Cyber-Physical Security Threats in Smart Grids
* **False Data Injection Attacks (FDIA):** An adversary compromises smart meters or substations to inject mathematically crafted false voltage and power readings. This tricks the automated state estimator into shutting down power generators or tripping transmission lines, triggering synthetic blackouts.
* **Vehicle-to-Grid (V2G) Storage:** Plug-in Electric Vehicles (PEVs) act as mobile batteries, charging when solar energy is abundant and feeding power back into the grid during evening peak demand.

---

## Key Exam Takeaways
* **PMU Time Synchronization:** Uses GPS satellite clock signals to synchronize phasor measurements across continental grids.
* **PMU Sampling Rate:** 30 to 60 samples per second (vastly faster than legacy SCADA).
* **MDMS Role:** Central utility backend processing smart meter data for billing and state estimation.
* **False Data Injection Attack (FDIA):** Tampering with sensor telemetry to fool power grid state estimators into false automatic trips.
* **V2G Concept:** Utilizing electric vehicle batteries as distributed grid energy storage.
