# Chapter 17: Sensor Networks — Part IV: Coverage and Connectivity

## Core Concept
Coverage and connectivity are the two primary quality-of-service metrics in wireless sensor networks. Coverage ensures every region or target of interest is effectively monitored by sensors, while connectivity guarantees that collected data can be routed over radio links back to the central sink node.

---

## Fundamental Parameters
* **Sensing Range (Rs):** The maximum radius within which a sensor can detect physical phenomena or events.
* **Communication Range (Rc):** The maximum distance over which a sensor node can reliably transmit radio packets to neighboring nodes.
* **The Relationship Rule:** If the communication range is at least twice the sensing range (Rc >= 2 * Rs), achieving complete area coverage automatically guarantees full network connectivity.

---

## Coverage Models
* **Area Coverage:** Every geometric point within a defined geographical region must be within the sensing range of at least one active sensor node.
* **Point Coverage:** Only a specific set of discrete stationary targets (such as individual wellheads or doorways) must be monitored by sensors.
* **Barrier Coverage:** Sensors are deployed along a perimeter or corridor to form a detection barrier, preventing an intruder from crossing the boundary undetected.
* **k-Coverage:** Every point or target is simultaneously monitored by at least k distinct sensor nodes, providing measurement redundancy and fault tolerance against node failure.

---

## Optimal Deployment Geometry
* To achieve complete area coverage with minimal overlap, sensors are arranged in a regular triangular lattice pattern.
* When all nodes share the same sensing range Rs, the optimal distance between adjacent active nodes is:
  * Optimal Distance = sqrt(3) * Rs (approximately 1.732 * Rs).
* This configuration covers the intersection points of circular sensing disks with the least wasted area, maximizing coverage efficiency and conserving node energy.

---

## Key Exam Takeaways
* **Connectivity Guarantee Rule:** Complete coverage guarantees connectivity when Rc >= 2 * Rs.
* **Barrier Coverage Goal:** Prevents undetected intrusion across a designated border or perimeter.
* **Optimal Separation Distance:** sqrt(3) * Rs between neighboring active nodes.
* **k-Coverage Purpose:** Ensures that each target is monitored by k independent sensors for redundancy.
* **Area vs. Point Coverage:** Area monitors an entire continuous surface, while point monitors discrete, specific locations.
